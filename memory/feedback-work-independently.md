Tal (2026-11-19): "why do you stop so many times and give me homework? I gave you access to my
chrome so you can work independently and JUST DO IT."
**Why:** each hand-back costs him a round trip; he had already authorized the task.
**How to apply:** once a task is authorized, carry it through in the browser: navigate, fill,
click Next, verify. Hand back only what you truly can't do: password/2FA entry, Meta popups that
open outside the Chrome tab group, or a click the safety check blocked. Then name the single
exact click and continue the moment he says done. Don't re-ask approval he already gave.
